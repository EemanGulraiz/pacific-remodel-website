452 BUNDY MODERN ESTATE

A flagship whole-home luxury transformation featuring custom marble culinary suites, vaulted transom glass living, primary spa bath, and signature concrete-turf paver grounds.

PROJECT CASE STUDY

THE VISION & ARCHITECTURAL EXECUTION

Located at 452 S Bundy Dr, Pacific Palisades / Brentwood, California, 452 Bundy was transformed from a traditional floorplan into a modern indoor-outdoor sanctuary. Pacific Remodel and Development directed the entire scope of work, focusing on structural reengineering, high-volume vaulted ceilings, and bespoke material selections.

Key architectural milestones included raising the living room roofline into a vaulted ceiling with triangular transom windows, crafting a custom marble waterfall island kitchen with rift oak millwork, creating a primary bathroom sanctuary with carved marble slab sinks, and constructing a seamless concrete-and-synthetic-turf grid driveway with motorized frosted glass security gates.

Custom Hardscaping: Integrated concrete paver grid with synthetic turf inlay.
Culinary Suite: Book-matched marble waterfall island & Thermador appliances.
Architectural Glass: High transom triangular glazing & pocketing patio doors.
Primary Spa: Carved light marble vanity, frameless shower & LED niche lighting.

2. VAULTED LIVING ROOM & TRANSOM GLASS

BEFORE • ORIGINAL LIVING
