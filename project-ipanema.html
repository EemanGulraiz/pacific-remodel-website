IPANEMA LUXURY RESIDENCE REMODEL

Ultra-luxury coastal apartment transformation in Ipanema, Rio de Janeiro, featuring open-concept oceanview living, custom timber millwork, gourmet kitchen, and primary suite.

INTERNATIONAL PROJECT CASE STUDY

PROJECT VISION & EXECUTION

Located in the iconic beachside enclave of Ipanema, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, this luxury villa remodel transformed a compartmentalized coastal property into a world-class luxury residence. Pacific Remodel and Development led full interior gut renovation, structural wall removal, custom cabinetry, and premium stone installation.

Key highlights include an expanded open-concept great room framing ocean views, a chef's kitchen with stone waterfall island, custom built-in timber shelving, a spa primary bath suite with rain shower, and a private sun terrace.
