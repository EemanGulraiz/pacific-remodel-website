12341 GORHAM CULINARY SUITE

Modern gourmet kitchen and interior overhaul featuring overhead architectural skylight, navy blue accent millwork, marble waterfall island, and marble spa bath suite.

PROJECT CASE STUDY

PROJECT VISION & EXECUTION

Located at 12341 Gorham Ave, Brentwood, California, this comprehensive interior remodel transformed the primary living and kitchen spaces into a sunlit culinary haven. Pacific Remodel and Development led structural alterations, skylight framing, custom cabinetry, and tilework.

Key highlights include an overhead glass skylight flooding the kitchen with natural sunlight, custom white upper cabinetry paired with deep navy blue lower drawers, a book-matched marble waterfall island, a luxury spa bathroom with marble tile shower surround, and a private courtyard patio decorated with string lights.
