4045 1/2 JACKSON MODERN RESIDENCE

Ground-up custom new construction residence in Culver City featuring custom deep teal high-gloss kitchen cabinetry, commercial range mantle, warm hardwood floors, and seamless garden views.

PROJECT CASE STUDY

PROJECT VISION & EXECUTION

Located at 4045 1/2 Jackson Ave, Culver City, California, this ground-up new construction modern residence combines open-concept architectural planning with rich interior finish selections.

The custom build features high-gloss deep teal kitchen cabinetry with gold-accent hardware, a commercial stainless steel range mantle, wide-plank hardwood flooring, contemporary bathroom vanities, and expansive garden view windows that fill every living space with natural light.
