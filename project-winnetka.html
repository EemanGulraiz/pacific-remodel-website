5336 WINNETKA ESTATE & POOL DECK

Complete whole-home remodel featuring an open-concept layout, white chef kitchen, covered outdoor lounge, and custom in-ground swimming pool deck.

PROJECT CASE STUDY

PROJECT VISION & EXECUTION

Located at 5336 Winnetka Ave, Woodland Hills, California, this impressive estate transformation upgraded both interior living quarters and the surrounding private outdoor grounds into a resort-style retreat.

Key highlights include a custom in-ground swimming pool deck surrounded by cypress trees and pea gravel paths, a covered outdoor lounge patio, a white chef kitchen with large marble island, an elegant entrance foyer, and open-concept dining and living quarters.
