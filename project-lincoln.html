4225 LINCOLN MODERN RESIDENCE

Full structural gut renovation featuring open-concept vaulted living quarters, white quartz gourmet chef kitchen, frameless glass bath sanctuary, and restored exterior facade.

PROJECT CASE STUDY

PROJECT VISION & EXECUTION

Located at 4225 Lincoln Ave, Los Angeles, California, this complete structural gut renovation transformed an aged, compartmentalized home into an open-concept architectural showpiece.

The construction scope involved stripping lath-and-plaster walls, reengineering roof trusses, upgrading main electrical panels, installing custom white quartz chef kitchen cabinetry, and designing a luxury marble bath suite.
