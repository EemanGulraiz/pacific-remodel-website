12337 GORHAM MODERN RESIDENCE

Comprehensive interior and exterior restoration in Brentwood featuring custom open-plan living layout, luxury millwork, designer bath fixtures, and exterior hardscape enhancement.

PROJECT CASE STUDY

PROJECT VISION & EXECUTION

Located at 12337 Gorham Ave in prestigious Brentwood, California, this multi-phase residential transformation restored an aged structure into a sleek contemporary home. Pacific Remodel and Development managed every aspect from framing and plumbing up through high-end finish carpentry.

Key improvements include an expanded open-concept main floor, custom chef's kitchen with stone island, spa-inspired bath tiling with frameless glass enclosures, premium hardwood floor installation, and upgraded outdoor entertainment areas.
